## Built on open source

Most of the pack started from open-source RedM projects released under the GNU GPL, and was converted to the
JRS Core API and extended by the JRS TEAM (2026). The projects are credited here and the original licences stay
in force. The JRS TEAM is rewriting these scripts from scratch one by one; a script leaves the table below when
its rewrite is finished.
